### Role and Context
You are a Senior Insurance Documentation Specialist and Technical Writer. You are tasked with creating a structural framework for insurance documentation that includes a high-level Global SOP and detailed Site Document Templates.

### Objective
Generate content for two distinct types of documents based on the requirements below.

---

### Part 1: Global SOP (Specification Table)
Create a **Global SOP** presented as a single, comprehensive table.

**Table Structure:**
* **Column 1: Insurance Sector** (Must cover exactly these three rows: Health Insurance, Vehicle Insurance, Life Insurance).
* **Column 2: Focus Areas** (List 5 to 7 key attributes per sector, e.g., Premiums, Waiting Periods, Exclusions, Claims Process, etc.).
* **Column 3: Insurance Document Titles** (List 3-4 specific document names relevant to that sector, e.g., "Covid Specific Policy," "Comprehensive Motor Policy," "Term Life Agreement").

---

### Part 2: Site Document Templates (The Actual Policy Documents)
Create a **Site Document Template** structure. This structure represents the actual terms and conditions.

**Requirements for Part 2:**
1.  **Standardization:** You must define a **Single Standard Table of Contents (TOC)** that logically applies to *all* three insurance sectors (Health, Vehicle, Life).
2.  **Content Generation:** For each of the three sectors (Health, Vehicle, Life), generate a sample document following the structure below:

**Document Structure:**
* **Section 1: Header**
    * Document Title (e.g., General Individual Health Insurance)
    * Company Name (e.g., HDFC Ergo, LIC, etc.)
* **Section 2: Master Table of Contents**
    * (Insert the standardized TOC you created here).
* **Section 3: Detailed Content**
    * For every item in the Master TOC, write specific content relevant to that specific Insurance Sector.
    * **Example:** If the TOC item is "Prior Document Submission," the Health section should mention medical history/surgery records, whereas the Vehicle section should mention registration/license documents.

**Numbering Format:**
Strictly use the following numbering format for the Detailed Content section:
`<Section Number>.<Point Number>` 
(e.g., 3.1, 3.2, 3.3...).

---

### Output Instructions
* Ensure the tone is professional, legally compliant, and formal.
* Present Part 1 (The Table) first, followed by Part 2 (The 3 Document Examples).
* Do not simply confirm you understand the prompt; please proceed directly with generating the content.

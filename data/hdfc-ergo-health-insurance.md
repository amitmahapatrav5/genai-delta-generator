### **Coverage Overview**

**1.1** Provides financial protection for hospitalization and medically necessary treatments as per approved policy conditions. \
**1.2** Includes cashless treatment at network hospitals subject to prior authorization. \
**1.3** Offers reimbursement-based claims for out-of-network treatments.

### **Eligibility & Enrollment**

**2.1** Adults aged 18–65 years are eligible for enrollment subject to underwriting.\
**2.2** Dependent children between 90 days and 25 years can be added under a family floater.\
**2.3** Enrollment requires compliance with KYC norms and acceptance of policy declarations.

### **Required Documentation**

**3.1** Previous medical history records including surgeries, chronic conditions, and medication details.\
**3.2** Diagnostic reports such as blood tests, ECG, or physician evaluation when required.\
**3.3** Identity proof, address proof, and prior health insurance policy documents.

### **Premiums & Payment Terms**

**4.1** Premium determined by age, medical background, sum insured, and optional riders selected.\
**4.2** Payment can be made annually, semi-annually, or monthly as per available modes.\
**4.3** Late payments processed within the grace period to maintain policy continuity.

### **Coverage Inclusions**

**5.1** In-patient hospitalization including room rent, doctor fees, and ICU expenses.\
**5.2** Pre-hospitalization (up to 30 days) and post-hospitalization (up to 60 days) coverage.\
**5.3** Day-care procedures that do not require 24-hour hospitalization.

### **Coverage Exclusions**

**6.1** Cosmetic or aesthetic treatments unless medically necessary due to accidents.\
**6.2** Treatment for pre-existing diseases during the initial waiting period.\
**6.3** Non-prescribed therapies, wellness treatments, or experimental procedures.

### **Claims Process**

**7.1** Notify insurer immediately upon hospitalization or planned treatment.\
**7.2** Submit claim form, hospital invoices, discharge summary, and treatment records.\
**7.3** For cashless treatment, obtain pre-authorization approval from the network hospital.
